## 2. Feature Dictionary & Engineering Pipeline

The model ingests 17 engineered pre-match features structured across market odds, team ratings, rolling form, territorial dominance, and player performance metrics. Crucially, all metrics are **strictly lagged** prior to kickoff to eliminate data leakage.

| Feature | Category | Description | Mathematical Definition / Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `p_home` | Market Odds | Overround-normalized implied probability of a Home Win. | $\frac{1 / \text{Odds}_H}{\sum_{k \in \{H,D,A\}} (1 / \text{Odds}_k)}$ |
| `p_draw` | Market Odds | Overround-normalized implied probability of a Draw. | $\frac{1 / \text{Odds}_D}{\sum_{k \in \{H,D,A\}} (1 / \text{Odds}_k)}$ |
| `p_away` | Market Odds | Overround-normalized implied probability of an Away Win. | $\frac{1 / \text{Odds}_A}{\sum_{k \in \{H,D,A\}} (1 / \text{Odds}_k)}$ |
| `elo_diff` | Team Rating | Pre-match ELO difference incorporating home field adjustment (+65 ELO). | $(\text{Elo}_{\text{home}} + 65.0) - \text{Elo}_{\text{away}}$ |
| `form_diff` | Recent Form | 5-match rolling points differential between opponents. | $\text{Points}_{\text{home, last 5}} - \text{Points}_{\text{away, last 5}}$ |
| `goals_form_diff` | Attacking Form | Differential in goals scored over the last 5 matches. | $\text{GF}_{\text{home, last 5}} - \text{GF}_{\text{away, last 5}}$ |
| `defensive_form_diff` | Defensive Form | Differential in goals conceded over last 5 matches (inverted so higher is better). | $\text{GA}_{\text{away, last 5}} - \text{GA}_{\text{home, last 5}}$ |
| `deep_diff` | Field Dominance | Differential in completed passes within 20m of the opponent goal line. | $\text{Deep}_{\text{home}} - \text{Deep}_{\text{away}}$ |
| `xpts_diff` | Chance Quality | Expected points differential estimated from cumulative expected goals ($xG$). | $\text{xPts}_{\text{home}} - \text{xPts}_{\text{away}}$ |
| `ppda_diff` | Tactical Pressing | Differential in Passes Allowed per Defensive Action in defensive 60% of pitch. | $\text{PPDA}_{\text{away}} - \text{PPDA}_{\text{home}}$ |
| `sot_diff` | Shot Accuracy | 5-match rolling differential in Shots on Target. | $\text{SoT}_{\text{home, avg}} - \text{SoT}_{\text{away, avg}}$ |
| `home_xg_avg` | Chance Creation | 5-match rolling mean of Expected Goals generated by the home team. | $\frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=1}^5 xG_{\text{home}, i}$ |
| `away_xg_avg` | Chance Creation | 5-match rolling mean of Expected Goals generated by the away team. | $\frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=1}^5 xG_{\text{away}, i}$ |
| `home_xga_avg` | Defensive Conceding | 5-match rolling mean of Expected Goals Conceded ($xGA$) by home team. | $\frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=1}^5 xGA_{\text{home}, i}$ |
| `away_xga_avg` | Defensive Conceding | 5-match rolling mean of Expected Goals Conceded ($xGA$) by away team. | $\frac{1}{5} \sum_{i=1}^5 xGA_{\text{away}, i}$ |
| `gk_performance_diff` | Goalkeeping | Differential in goalkeeper save percentage and post-shot $xG$ prevented. | $\text{GK}_{\text{home}} - \text{GK}_{\text{away}}$ |
| `shooting_efficiency_diff` | Clinical Finishing | Differential in shot accuracy ($\text{SoT}/\text{Shots}$) and goal conversion ($\text{Goals}/\text{Shots}$). | $\text{Finishing}_{\text{home}} - \text{Finishing}_{\text{away}}$ |

## 4. Probabilistic Quality & Calibration Metrics

Accurate probabilities are essential for sports forecasting and simulation. A model that outputs 59% accuracy but poorly calibrated probabilities will produce wildly inaccurate Monte Carlo season projections.

### 4.1 Calibration & Probabilistic Scoring
1. **Multi-Class Log Loss (Cross-Entropy)**:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\log} = -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{k=1}^K y_{i,k} \ln(\hat{p}_{i,k}) = \mathbf{0.8665}$$
   *Outperforms the uncalibrated model ($\mathcal{L}_{\log} = 0.9832$) and naive prior baseline ($\mathcal{L}_{\log} = 1.0986$).*

2. **Multiclass Brier Score**:
   $$\text{BS} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{k=1}^K (\hat{p}_{i,k} - y_{i,k})^2 = \mathbf{0.5089}$$
   *A strictly proper scoring rule penalizing overconfident incorrect predictions.*

3. **Macro ROC-AUC (One-vs-Rest)**:
   $$\text{AUC}_{\text{macro}} = \mathbf{0.7659}$$
   * **Home Win vs Rest**: **0.8124**
   * **Away Win vs Rest**: **0.7891**
   * **Draw vs Rest**: **0.6962**

4. **Inter-Rater & Correlation Metrics**:
   * **Cohen’s Kappa ($\kappa$)**: **0.3492** (Substantial agreement accounting for chance)
   * **Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC)**: **0.3663** (Symmetric metric robust to class imbalance)

### 4.2 Expected Calibration Error (ECE) & Temperature Scaling
Raw gradient boosting tree softmax scores tend to be overconfident near the extremes ($p \to 0$ or $p \to 1$). We applied **Temperature Scaling** post-processing:

$$\hat{q}_{i,k} = \frac{\exp(z_{i,k} / T)}{\sum_{j=1}^K \exp(z_{i,j} / T)}$$

Where $T$ is optimized on a validation holdout using negative log-likelihood minimization:
- **Optimal Temperature Parameter**: $T^* = 0.754$
- **Uncalibrated Model ECE**: `0.0482`
- **Temperature Scaled ECE**: `0.0331` (31.3% error reduction)
- **Bookmaker Implied Odds Benchmark ECE**: `0.0360` (Our model achieves lower calibration error than the bookmaker market)

## 6. Monte Carlo Season Simulation (10,000 Iterations)

To forecast the full 380-match season, a Monte Carlo engine was constructed to simulate the remaining 292 unplayed fixtures across 10,000 independent season trajectories.

### 6.1 Simulation Dynamics
- Each unplayed fixture $(i, j)$ samples an outcome directly from the calibrated probability distribution:
  $$Y_{i,j} \sim \text{Categorical}(\hat{p}_{H}, \hat{p}_{D}, \hat{p}_{A})$$
- Goals are generated via bi-variate Poisson distributions parameterized by team expected goals:
  $$G_{\text{home}} \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda_{\text{home}}), \quad G_{\text{away}} \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda_{\text{away}})$$
  $$\lambda_{\text{home}} = \bar{\lambda} \cdot \alpha_{\text{home}} \cdot \beta_{\text{away}} \cdot \gamma_{\text{home-adv}}$$
- Accumulated points and goal difference are aggregated per iteration to compute title, top 4, and relegation distributions.

## 8. Technical Architecture Decisions & Methodological Justifications

### 8.1 Model Selection Rationale: Gradient Boosted Trees vs. Deep Neural Networks
Tabular sports datasets inherently feature heterogeneous feature distributions (bounded market probabilities, continuous ELO differentials, and discrete rolling goal tallies). Tree-based gradient boosting models consistently outperform neural networks on tabular datasets (Grinsztajn et al., *NeurIPS 2022*). XGBoost natively handles irregular distributions, prevents overfitting via shrinkage ($\eta = 0.05$) and $L_1/L_2$ regularization ($\alpha = 0.1, \lambda = 1.0$), and provides deterministic tree-structure interpretability via SHAP.

### 8.2 Modeling Draw Equilibrium in Low-Scoring Match Dynamics
In association football, draws represent an equilibrium state rather than an intentional target state. Matches frequently enter draw states due to low scoring frequency (Poisson $\lambda \approx 1.35$ goals/team). While naive models predict exclusively Home or Away wins to artificially inflate accuracy, the calibrated XGBoost model achieves **40.0% precision** on draws by isolating tactical parity conditions in pressing intensity (`ppda_diff`) and chance quality (`xpts_diff`).

### 8.3 Post-Hoc Probability Calibration via Temperature Scaling
Standard gradient boosting tree ensembles optimize for multi-class cross-entropy, which frequently leads to overconfident probability estimates near 0 and 1. Temperature scaling applies a single learned parameter $T > 0$ ($T^* \approx 0.754$) to soften the logit vector without altering class rankings ($\text{argmax}$). This reduces Expected Calibration Error (ECE) from 0.048 to 0.033, ensuring the predicted probabilities accurately reflect empirical frequencies for downstream simulation.

### 8.4 Preserving Realistic Variance in 10,000 Monte Carlo Iterations
Rather than projecting static point expectations, each unplayed fixture outcome is sampled from the calibrated XGBoost categorical distribution $\text{Categorical}(p_H, p_D, p_A)$. Goal scoring is modeled using bi-variate Poisson distributions incorporating team attack and defense ratings. Running 10,000 full-season trajectories generates realistic point distributions ($\sigma \approx 5.5$ PTS) and empirically validated 90% confidence intervals.

### 8.5 Strict Causal Lagging to Prevent Temporal Data Leakage
All rolling features (`form_diff`, `home_xg_avg`, `goals_form_diff`) are calculated using strictly lagged historical windows ($\text{Match}_{t-1}$ to $\text{Match}_{t-5}$). A fixture occurring on Matchday 9 only has access to matches completed on or before Matchday 8. Furthermore, ELO ratings are updated strictly post-match using standard K-factor updating ($K=32$).
